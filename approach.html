For Project Owners

Every capital project starts with a question: what does our organization actually need, and is a building even the right answer? Placekeep gets involved at that question, before design, before construction, sometimes before you're sure you want to build anything at all. We listen first, and we'll tell you honestly if a building isn't the answer, even if that means the project ends there.

Once a path forward is clear, the coordination begins, and for Tribal and public entities, nonprofits, and mission-driven organizations, that's real work across your whole organization. It's not always intuitive, and it rarely moves in a straight line.

The categories below show where we typically plug in. Every engagement is different, so think of this as a menu, not a sequence.

Six ways we partner with you:

Before the Building:

Most projects start long before anyone agrees on what the project actually is.

Starting with the Real Problem: We start wherever you are, even if all you know is that something isn't working.

A First Step: A short, fixed-fee engagement that turns "something's wrong" into a clear direction, before a full feasibility study or design process begins.

Asking the Right Questions: We help you understand what's actually driving the problem, space, capacity, funding, or something else entirely, before any solution gets proposed.

For Design Firms

Every Tribal, nonprofit, mission-driven, or public project carries community and cultural context, and most design teams don't do that kind of work every day.

Placekeep works alongside your team at whatever depth a project needs. Sometimes that's a set of findings to design from. Other times it's us in the room through concept. The three tiers below show what that can look like.

Each tier is a self-contained way to bring us in. Most firms start at Tier 1. Tiers 2 and 3 get added when a partnership is already working.

Three ways to bring us in:

Grounding:

For firms that don't have community engagement in-house, or don't want to build it out for one project.

Engagement & Research: We handle early and stakeholder engagement, cultural research, site and context analysis, and facilitation.

Live Handoff: We deliver findings in a live working session on a shared board that stays open as a reference for the rest of the project.

You Design From It: Your team designs from what we found.
